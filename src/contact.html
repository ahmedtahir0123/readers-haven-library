Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some common questions about our library.

How can I become a library member?

You can visit our library and complete the membership registration form. Our staff will guide you through the process.

Is library membership free?

We offer different membership plans. Please visit our Membership page to explore the available options.

How many books can I borrow at a time?

Members can borrow up to three books at a time, depending on their membership plan.

Can I renew my borrowed books?

Yes, you can request a renewal before the due date, subject to book availability and library policies.

Does the library provide digital services?

Yes, we provide access to digital books, online reading resources, and other digital learning materials.

What should I do if I lose a library book?

Please contact our library staff as soon as possible. They will guide you according to our replacement policy.

Can I visit the library without membership?

Yes, visitors are welcome to explore our library. However, borrowing books requires membership.
