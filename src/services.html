Online Book Catalogue

Browse our complete inventory of physical books, academic journals, and magazines from anywhere before you visit.

Availability: 24/7 Online Portal
  1. Open the Readers' Haven catalogue portal in any browser.
  2. Search by book title, author, ISBN, or subject category.
  3. Reserve available copies with your member ID for 48-hour pickup.
📱

E-Books & Audiobooks

Borrow digital editions and narrated audiobooks directly on your smartphone, tablet, or e-reader with automatic returns.

Loan Period: 14 Days (No late fees)
  1. Sign in to the Readers' Haven Digital App with your library card.
  2. Browse or filter e-books and tap "Borrow".
  3. Download titles for offline reading on up to 3 devices.
🧑‍💻

Study-Room Booking

Reserve quiet, soundproof rooms equipped with whiteboards, power outlets, and seating for solo focus or group projects.

Timings: Mon–Fri 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM | Sat 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  1. Choose a Solo Pod or 4–6 Person Group Room online or at reception.
  2. Book a 1-hour or 2-hour slot up to 7 days in advance.
  3. Check in at the Help Desk within 15 minutes of your start time.
🖨️

Printing & Scanning

Self-service black-and-white or color printing, photocopying, and complimentary document scanning to USB or email.

Timings: Mon–Sat, 9:30 AM – 7:30 PM
  1. Upload your PDF at the Print Kiosk or plug in your USB drive.
  2. Select B&W (PKR 10/page), Color (PKR 30/page), or Free Scan.
  3. Pay at the counter or tap your prepaid library card to print.
📶

Free High-Speed Wi-Fi

Complimentary fiber-optic internet access available across all reading halls, research desks, and study lounges.

Availability: During all library operating hours
  1. Connect your device to the ReadersHaven-Guest Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open your web browser to load the library login portal.
  3. Sign in with your membership ID or request a daily guest pass at reception.
