REGISTRATION PROCESS

How to Apply in 4 Easy Steps

Need Help? Contact Us →
01

Pick Your Plan

Select Student, Adult, or Family membership based on your borrowing needs.

02

Submit Documents

Bring your filled form and required ID documents to the Membership Desk.

03

Pay Annual Fee

Pay your annual fee via cash, debit/credit card, or bank transfer.

04

Collect Your Card

Receive your printed Library Card in 15–20 minutes and activate online access.

📄

Required Documents

  • Copy of valid CNIC or B-Form (for applicants under 18)
  • Valid Student ID card (mandatory for Student Membership)
  • Two recent passport-size photographs
  • Proof of local residence (utility bill or hostel letter)
🕒

Processing Timings & Renewal

Saturday 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
